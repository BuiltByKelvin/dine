Our story

A story told in fire.

Ìtàn means "story" in Yoruba. Ours begins at dawn, at Mile 12 market, choosing peppers by scent and heft — tatashe, rodo, shombo — the way our grandmothers did.

We cook over open fire and clay. We smoke, we ferment, we age. Then we plate it in a way that respects where it came from — and where Nigerian food is going next.

Every dish on this menu has a name, a place, and a person behind it. Ask us about any of them.

Chef Adéṣínà Ògúnlànà
